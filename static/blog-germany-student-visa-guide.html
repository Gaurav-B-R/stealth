On the Studienkolleg route, fund at the student-applicant rate. The blocked-account figure most guides quote — around €11,904 for the year, about €992 a month — applies to students with a place on a degree programme. If you enter as a student applicant (Studienbewerber) under §17(2) AufenthG, which is how a Studienkolleg or preparatory year is normally covered, you have to show €1,091 a month, €13,092 for the year — €1,188 more than the standard figure, so funding at the standard rate leaves your proof of financing short. The Federal Foreign Office sets out the blocked-account rules, but the amount for your route is published by the mission handling your application — confirm it there before you open the account.

Still choosing a program? Stage 2 starts with an admission, and Rilono's AI university shortlist is how you get there: give it your field, budget, grades and language scores and it returns a deliberate mix of reach, match and safety programs, each with an estimated annual tuition range, the entry requirements you'll be measured against and, where the data is available, its QS world rank, national rank and application fee. Tuition works differently in Germany, and "tuition-free" is getting less true every intake. Many public universities still charge nothing beyond a per-semester contribution, but the fees that do exist land on non-EU students: Baden-Württemberg charges them €1,500 per semester, and Bavaria has followed. For non-EU students enrolling from winter semester 2024/25, TUM charges €2,000–3,000 per semester on bachelor's programmes and €4,000–6,000 on master's; TH Nürnberg introduces €1,000 per semester from winter semester 2026/27, and FAU from summer semester 2027. Exemptions exist and some programmes stay fee-free, so treat any tuition figure as an estimate, check each program's official fee page for the exact amount and whether your intake is covered, and use the shortlist for fit, ranks and entry requirements. Results are grounded in live web search, and any link goes to the university's own admissions page, never a third-party aggregator.

Step 5 — Prepare for the visa interview

Whether you are interviewed varies by mission: some German missions question student applicants, others decide on the file alone, so prepare as though yours will. Expect questions about why this program and university, how your studies fit your background, how you'll fund your stay, and your plans after graduating. Use Rilono's interview-prep sessions to rehearse realistic questions, and AI mock interviews (including full voice mock interviews on the Visa Success Pass) to practice under pressure and get scored, specific feedback.

Efficient loop: run a mock, read the feedback, fix your weakest two answers, run another. What officers look for is a consistent story — your program choice, your finances and your study plan all lining up with your documents.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a blocked account (Sperrkonto), and how much money goes in it?+

Most students prove financing with a blocked account at an approved provider — recently around €11,904 for one year (about €992/month), though scholarships or a formal sponsor declaration (Verpflichtungserklärung) can work instead. The rate is higher if you travel as a student applicant (Studienbewerber) under §17(2) AufenthG, which covers the Studienkolleg route: €1,091 a month, €13,092 for the year. The amounts are updated periodically — check the Federal Foreign Office blocked-account rules and confirm the figure for your route with the German mission handling your application before you open the account.

What is the APS certificate and do I need one?+

The APS (Akademische Prüfstelle) pre-checks the authenticity of your academic documents. Applicants from India, China, Vietnam and Mongolia generally need an APS certificate before applying for a German student visa. It can take weeks, so start early. For the summer semester 2027 Master's intake, Indian applicants whose previous degree falls in Engineering, Commerce / Accounting / Finance / Economics or Business / Management must also add a dMAT certificate (a €150 aptitude test administered by g.a.s.t.) to their APS documentation, with registration closing 15 September 2026 — unless they registered for APS online, or shipped their APS documents, before 29 June 2026. Confirm whether it applies to you on your local German mission's website.

Is the Type D visa the same as my German residence permit?+

No. The national Type D visa gets you into Germany. After arriving you register your address (Anmeldung) and then apply to the local foreigners' office (Ausländerbehörde) for a student residence permit before the visa expires. Rilono's final journey stage tracks exactly these arrival steps.

Do I need to speak German to get the visa?+

It depends on your course language. German-taught programs generally expect TestDaF, DSH or an equivalent certificate; English-taught programs accept IELTS/TOEFL or proof of English instruction. Upload whichever certificate matches your program and Rilono checks it against your admission.

Will Rilono fill or submit my visa application for me?+

No. Rilono Copilot is read-only — it guides you field by field (including in the Consular Services Portal, where VIDEX now sits) but never types or submits. You stay in control of every field on your application. Rilono also provides document-organization and AI guidance tools, not legal or immigration advice — always confirm requirements with the German mission responsible for you.
