FAQ

What consultancies ask before they switch.

Straight answers on pricing, credits, team access, enquiry capture and how current the university data is.

What does Rilono Enterprise cost? +
Plans are Starter at ₹2,999/month, Growth at ₹6,999/month and Scale at ₹14,999/month, all exclusive of GST. They cover 10, 30 and 100 users and 100, 500 and 2,000 active clients respectively, and each includes a monthly AI credit allowance of 1,000, 3,500 and 10,000 credits. Every workspace starts on a free 14-day self-serve sandbox with 2 users, 10 active clients and 100 demo credits, and no card.
Are AI credits included in the plan or bought separately? +
Both. Your plan grants its credit allowance to your wallet at the start of every billing period, and that is what normal use runs on. If a busy month exhausts it you can top up at any time. Unused plan credits do not carry into the next period; top-up credits you purchase never expire.
Does browsing the course catalog cost credits? +
No. Browsing universities and courses is free. Credits are only debited when you generate an AI shortlist, and a generation that fails or returns nothing is never charged.
Who on my team can see the Leads inbox? +
An enquiry arrives with no branch and no counselor attached, so the inbox is visible to members whose access scope is the entire workspace. Branch-scoped and caseload-scoped members work from their assigned clients instead.
Can I take enquiries straight from my website? +
Yes. Each form has a shareable link you can put on your site, socials or ads. Submissions land in your Leads inbox with their utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign or referrer recorded, and you can pause or rotate the link at any time.
How current is the shortlist data? +
AI shortlists are grounded in live web search rather than stale model memory, and catalog entries carry the date they were last verified — a filter narrows the catalog to verified entries only. Tuition and fee figures are estimates, so always confirm on the university's own admissions page, which recommendations link to where one is available.
Which destinations does the course catalog cover? +
The course catalog covers the US, UK, Canada, Australia and Germany. The CRM itself supports client journeys to Ireland as well.
