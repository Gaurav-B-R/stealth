Questions, answered

Is Rilono free? +
Yes — you can create an account and start organizing your visa journey for free, including 3 DS-160 auto-fills and 1 document red-flag scan. To unlock unlimited document audits, full red-flag reveals and Rilono AI voice mock interviews, get the Visa Success Pass — a one-time ₹999 for 30 days (no subscription).
Which visas does Rilono support? +
Rilono focuses on US student visas — F-1, J-1 and M-1 — with document guidance, validation and interview prep tailored to each. Consultancies using Rilono Enterprise can manage student visas across the US, Canada, UK, Australia, Germany and Ireland.
Is my data secure? +
Absolutely. Your documents are protected with zero-knowledge encryption, meaning they're encrypted with a key derived from your password. Not even our team can read your files.
Does Rilono help with the visa interview? +
Yes. Rilono includes AI-powered mock interviews with real consulate-style questions for your destination and instant feedback, so you walk in prepared and confident.
I run a visa consultancy — can I use Rilono? +
Definitely. Rilono Enterprise is a complete CRM for study-abroad consultancies: manage every student, track their visa status through a visual pipeline, keep notes, send emails, and see everything on one dashboard. Explore Enterprise →
