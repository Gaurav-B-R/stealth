UK Student visa maintenance funds calculator

The maintenance amount is the single most confusing part of a UK Student visa — London vs outside London, months of living costs, unpaid tuition and the 28-day rule. Enter your details below to get the exact figure and the dates your bank balance must cover.

How UK maintenance funds work

  • Living costs. Currently £1,529/month in London and £1,171/month outside London, for up to 9 months.
  • Tuition. Add your first-year (or full course, if under a year) tuition, minus anything you've already paid.
  • Accommodation offset. Up to £1,334 of accommodation paid to your university reduces the amount you must show.
  • The 28-day rule. The total must sit in your account for 28 consecutive days without dipping below it, and your statement's closing date must be within 31 days of your application.
