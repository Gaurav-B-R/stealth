What Rilono AI does for Subclass 500 applicants

Rilono AI isn't a generic chatbot. It's grounded in your data — your profile, your uploaded documents, and exactly where you are in the Subclass 500 process — so its guidance is specific to you. Its core job is mismatch-first review: before it suggests your next step, it cross-checks your profile and documents for contradictions (a name spelled differently on your passport and CoE, a funding gap, OSHC dates that don't cover your whole course) and flags those first. That's the difference between "here's some general advice" and "your OSHC ends two months before your course does — extend it before you lodge."

Step 4 — Chat with Rilono AI the right way

Rilono AI already has your profile, documents and current stage in context — you don't need to re-explain your situation. Because it reviews for mismatches first, the most valuable way to use it is to ask it to check, not just to tell. Prompts that work well:

"Cross-check my passport, CoE and application for any name, date or course mismatches before I lodge."

"Based on my documents, what is my single next step and what am I still missing?"

"Does my OSHC cover my entire course dates, and does my financial evidence meet the current requirement?"

"Review my Genuine Student answers for consistency with my study plan and finances."

Ask one focused question at a time, and act on the flagged mismatches before asking "what's next" — fixing inconsistencies early is exactly what prevents a refusal over an avoidable gap.

Step 5 — Sharpen your Genuine Student responses

Under the Subclass 500, your Genuine Student (GS) answers carry real weight — targeted questions about why you chose your course, how it fits your background and career, your ties, and how you'll meet your visa conditions. Use Rilono AI to draft, critique and tighten each answer so your course choice, finances and intentions tell one consistent story. Efficient loop: draft an answer, ask Rilono to critique it against your documents, fix the weakest points, and re-check. Consistency across your GS answers, CoE and finances is exactly what case officers assess.

Frequently asked questions

Do I still need a GTE statement?+

No. The Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) statement was replaced by the Genuine Student (GS) requirement on 23 March 2024. It's now built into your visa application as targeted questions (each with a word limit) about your course choice, background and plans, rather than a separate statement. Always confirm current rules on the official Department of Home Affairs source.

How much money do I have to show?+

The Department of Home Affairs sets a living-cost benchmark (from 10 May 2024, AUD 29,710 per year for a single applicant) that you must show on top of tuition and travel costs. Rilono keeps your checklist pointed at the current figure and AI-checks your bank and sponsor documents against it. Always confirm the latest amount on the official source before you rely on it.

Do I need OSHC before I apply?+

Yes. Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) is mandatory (visa condition 8501) and must span your entire intended stay, from an Australian government-approved insurer, before you lodge your application. Rilono tracks your cover dates so they never fall short of your CoE.

Will Rilono fill or submit my ImmiAccount application for me?+

No. Rilono Copilot is read-only — it guides you field by field but never types or submits. You stay in control of every field on your ImmiAccount application.

Does Rilono give legal or immigration advice?+

No. Rilono provides document-organization and AI guidance tools, not legal or immigration advice. Australian immigration rules change often — always confirm requirements with the official Department of Home Affairs source.
