FAQ

Common questions

Is the demo really free and no-obligation?

Yes. It's a no-pressure walkthrough. There's no card, no contract, and you can start on the free core CRM afterwards whenever you like.

How long does the demo take?

Around an hour — a full, personalized walkthrough with plenty of time for your questions. We tailor it to your countries, team size and workflow rather than reading from a script.

What does it cost?

The core CRM is free to run. Premium AI actions (Deep Scans, AI mock interviews) use prepaid Rilono Credits — pay-as-you-go, no seat fees. We'll walk you through the exact numbers in the demo.

Is our students' data secure?

Yes. Documents are encrypted at rest and each consultancy's data is tenant-isolated. Security and confidentiality are core to how the platform is built.

Can we use our own branding?

Absolutely — you run Rilono Enterprise on your own subdomain with your logo, so your clients only ever see your brand.
